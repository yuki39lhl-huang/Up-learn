# 2024年广东省专升本《大学英语》真题答案与解析

## 第一部分 阅读

### 第一节（每题2分，共30分）

#### A 篇

**1. At Organic EcoPark, visitors can ______**
**答案：B（ride horses）**
**解析：** 原文提到“riding horses, feeding fish, picking vegetables and mushrooms”，A 是 bouncy castle，不是 build a castle；C 是 picking vegetables，不是 plant；D 是 workshop 相关内容，不是 make drinks。

**2. Which of the following suits people who have a dog?**
**答案：A（Go Green Farm）**
**解析：** 原文 Go Green Farm 提到“it is pet-friendly. Your four-legged friends can have a good time there.”

**3. What makes the trip to Sunrise Village special?**
**答案：D（Learning about the rice production）**
**解析：** 原文提到“you will get a chance to know the entire rice production process. It will make your trip special.”

**4. What do visitors need to do before going to Butterfly Valley?**
**答案：C（Book in advance）**
**解析：** 原文“The farm doesn't take walk-ins, so you need to make a reservation before heading out there.”

**5. How much does it cost a parent with two kids to visit Holiday Farm?**
**答案：B（$70）**
**解析：** 成人 $30，儿童每人 $20，$30 + $20 × 2 = $70。

#### B 篇

**6. How does the lack of windows affect students?**
**答案：C（It shortens their attention span.）**
**解析：** 原文“It makes their concentration span shorter.”

**7. Hayden brought nature into classrooms by ______**
**答案：D（printing the tree photos onto the ceiling）**
**解析：** 原文“Then he got them printed onto the ceilings.”

**8. The change in the classrooms led to the students ______**
**答案：B（increased attendance）**
**解析：** 原文“Overall, the class attendance has gone up because students are happier...”

**9. What is David's attitude toward the addition?**
**答案：A（Favorable）**
**解析：** David 说“It is pretty great and beautiful. When I look up, it feels like I am sitting under a tree.” 说明态度积极。

**10. What is the main idea of the passage?**
**答案：D（Tree ceilings helped students perform better.）**
**解析：** 全文讲述 Hayden 将树照片印在天花板上，改善学生情绪、注意力和出勤，帮助其表现更好。

#### C 篇

**11. According to Paragraph 1, a major event in animal history was that ______**
**答案：B（birds developed the ability to fly）**
**解析：** 原文“Birds gained the ability to fly about 150 million years ago, which was a major event animal history.”

**12. What is the second paragraph mainly about?**
**答案：A（The key process of the research.）**
**解析：** 第二段介绍研究过程：对现代鸟类进行脑部扫描，比较飞行前后脑活动。

**13. The researchers studied bird-like dinosaurs ______**
**答案：D（to find what their cerebells were like）**
**解析：** 原文“the research team needed to figure out what the cerebellum looked like in creatures that were alive around the time when birds began to fly.”

**14. What does the underlined word “deteriorated” in Paragraph 4 probably mean?**
**答案：C（were damaged）**
**解析：** 大脑早已腐烂/损坏，所以只能研究颅骨。“deteriorated”意为“恶化、损坏”。

**15. In which section of a magazine does this passage most likely appear?**
**答案：C（Science）**
**解析：** 文章涉及鸟类飞行、小脑、脑扫描、恐龙颅骨等科学研究内容。
